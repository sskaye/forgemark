# Code of conduct

Forgemark is built in the open, by people who don't all know each other. To keep it a place where anyone can ask a question, report a bug or send a change:

- Be considerate and patient. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Criticism of code, docs and ideas is welcome; personal attacks aren't.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content, or unwelcome attention, toward anyone, for any reason.
- Don't publish anyone's private information.
- Stay on topic. Issues, pull requests and discussions are for Forgemark.

This applies to everything in this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit messages.

## If something goes wrong

If someone's behaviour here is a problem, report it to GitHub with **Report content** (the ⋯ menu on any comment, issue or pull request). For abuse or spam, GitHub's [reporting page](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) reaches its staff directly. You can also open an issue asking the maintainer to look at a thread, without repeating what was said.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, and block anyone who doesn't follow this, from the repository or from the project for good. Decisions are made fairly, and a report is never held against the person who made it.

This is adapted, in short, from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
